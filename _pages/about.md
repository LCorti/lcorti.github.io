I am a currently PhD Candidate in the [Web Information Systems (WIS)](https://www.wis.ewi.tudelft.nl/) group at TU Delft. There, I am supervised by Dr. [Jie Yang](https://yangjiera.github.io/) and Prof.Dr.Ir. [Geert-Jan Houben](https://www.wis.ewi.tudelft.nl/houben).

I research and develop **methods** and **tools** to diagnose large vision and language models. My work bridges Natural Language Processing and model Interpretability to specify desirable model behaviours and verify observable ones.

Before starting my PhD, I worked as a Research Assistant at Politecnico di Milano. 
I received my MSc and BSc in Computer Science and Engineering from Politecnico di Milano in 2019 and 2017, respectively.
