I work on **tools** and **methods** for diagnosing large vision and language models. My work sits at the intersection of NLP and interpretable ML, where I develop rigorous evaluations and integrate modern vision and language models into end-to-end applications. I have hands-on experience with frameworks such as PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, and spaCy, and I am particularly interested in building reliable and robust multimodal systems and workflows.

I am currently pursuing a PhD at Delft University of Technology, supervised by [Jie Yang](https://yangjiera.github.io/) and [Geert-Jan Houben](https://www.wis.ewi.tudelft.nl/houben). Previously, I worked as a Research Assistant at Politecnico di Milano, where I also received my MSc and BSc in Computer Science and Engineering.
